A cozy table, a clear privacy promise.

Privacy information for Tavolunio, a free cooperative restaurant game for Android.

Current status: pre-release prototype. This page describes the features present today. It will be updated before any online service is released.

What the current prototype does

The prototype provides offline solo play and local-network cooperative testing. It does not currently include online matchmaking, Firebase, an account, advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, chat, or third-party tracking.

Information processed on your device

Game settings and any local progress are stored on the device. The game does not send these local records to the publisher. Android and Google Play may process information under their own services and policies.

Local cooperative play

When local-network multiplayer is used, the participating devices exchange the live game state needed for the session, such as player movement and service actions, over the local connection. Do not enter personal information in a room name or other game field. The current prototype has no public player directory or text/voice chat.

Children and audience

Tavolunio is designed for a general audience of teenagers and adults and is not specifically directed to children under 13.

Future online features

Internet matchmaking or cloud services are not available in this prototype. If they are added, this notice will be revised before release to describe the service, data, retention, and choices accurately.
