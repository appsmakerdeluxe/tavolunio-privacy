What the prototype does

Solo play works offline. The multiplayer menu can create online rooms and join them with a six-character code or invite link. Room creation and joining use Firebase with anonymous authentication. A shared live game session is still in development.

Data used for online rooms

Firebase creates a random user identifier (UID) for anonymous authentication; the game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location. A room code, host/guest UIDs, role, creation time, and expiry time are stored in Firebase Realtime Database (europe-west1 region). Firebase may process technical connection data such as your IP address to provide the service. Do not put personal information in a room code or game field.

Storage and your choices

A room code stops accepting joins after one hour. This currently does not automatically delete the room record from the database, so it may remain until cleanup. The anonymous Firebase identifier is stored on the device and remains with Firebase until deleted. For deletion requests, contact us below. You can play solo offline and avoid multiplayer. Local settings and progress stay on your device.

Other information

The game currently has no ads, in-app purchases, analytics, chat, or third-party tracking. This website uses no trackers, cookies, or externally loaded fonts. Android, Google Play, and Firebase may process information under their own privacy policies. Tavolunio is intended for a general audience of teenagers and adults and is not specifically directed to children under 13.
